01 Metal scrap

From tangled scrap to clean ingots.

Ferrous or non-ferrous, loose or baled — we weigh it, grade it and pay on the spot. Every load is sorted by alloy with handheld XRF, sheared, shredded and melted into certified ingots ready for the foundry.

02 Printed circuit boards

An urban mine, layer by layer.

A circuit board is the richest ore on earth. We depopulate components, shred the laminate and separate it into copper, precious-metal concentrate and inert glass fibre — with full data destruction and a documented chain of custody.

  • Gold
  • Silver
  • Palladium
  • Copper
  • Tin
  • Tantalum
Richer than oreTonne for tonne, circuit boards carry far more gold than the rock that is mined for it.

03 Wire & cable

Strip the jacket. Keep the copper.

House wire, harnesses, armoured cable, data cable — we granulate it and separate the metal from the insulation by density, without burning a thing. Out comes bright copper and aluminium chop; the plastics go on to be reused too.

  • Power cable
  • Harnesses
  • Data cable
  • Armoured
  • Enamelled

01

Collect

Bins, skips and scheduled pickups from factories, workshops, demolition sites and IT rooms. Every load is weighed on a calibrated bridge.

02

Sort

Magnets, eddy currents, sensors and trained hands split the stream by metal, alloy and grade.

03

Shred

Shears, shredders and granulators reduce everything to a uniform size and liberate metal from plastic, glass and resin.

04

Separate

Density tables, electrostatics and air classifiers pull clean copper, aluminium and precious-metal fractions out of the mix.

05

Refine

Melted, assayed and cast. The metal leaves us as certified raw material — and goes straight back into things people make.
