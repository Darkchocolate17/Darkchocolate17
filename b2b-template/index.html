Questions

Asked before every contract.

Do our suppliers have to pay or install anything?

No. Suppliers join free from an emailed link and work in the browser. Larger suppliers can connect their own ERP at no charge.

Which ERP systems do you connect to?

SAP S/4HANA and ECC, Oracle Fusion, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and NetSuite have certified connectors. Anything else connects through the REST API or flat-file exchange.

Where is our data stored?

In the region you choose, the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we hold ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II reports.

What happens if we go over our order volume?

Nothing stops. We bill the overage at the same per-order rate as your plan and suggest the next tier if it would cost you less.
